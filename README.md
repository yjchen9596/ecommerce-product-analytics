# E-commerce Product Growth & Retention Analytics

## Project Overview

This is an end-to-end product analytics project focused on understanding e-commerce customer behavior and identifying opportunities to improve conversion, retention, and revenue.

Using real-world e-commerce event data, this project explores customer journeys, purchasing behavior, conversion funnels, and retention patterns through exploratory data analysis, behavioral analytics, and predictive modeling.

**Dataset:** [Kaggle – eCommerce Behavior Data from Multi-Category Store](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mkechinov/ecommerce-behavior-data-from-multi-category-store)

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses need to understand how customers interact with their platforms, what drives purchasing decisions, and why customers abandon their shopping journeys.

**Main Business Question:**

How can an e-commerce company leverage user behavioral data to identify opportunities to improve conversion rates, customer retention, and revenue?

## Key Analytical Questions

1. **User Behavior:** How do customers interact with products, categories, and shopping experiences?
2. **Conversion Funnel:** Where do customers drop off between viewing a product, adding it to their cart, and completing a purchase?
3. **Customer Retention:** How frequently do customers return, and how does retention vary across customer segments?
4. **Revenue & Growth:** Which customer behaviors, product categories, and purchasing patterns are associated with revenue generation?
5. **Predictive Analytics:** Can historical behavioral patterns help predict future customer purchasing or retention?
